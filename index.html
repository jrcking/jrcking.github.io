I am a Research Fellow working in the School of Engineering at the University of Manchester. My research focusses on high-fidelity simulations of fluids. A few of the areas I enjoy working in are:

  • the development of novel numerical methods, particularly mesh-free and high-order methods;
  • simulations of fluids with complex rheology;
  • visocelastic flow instabilities and transitions;
  • combustion instabilities.


My research is currently funded by the Royal Society and the Leverhulme Trust. For information on some research highlights, see research, and for a list of publications, see publications.

I lead the Complex Fluids Group at the University of Manchester - see our website for more details of who we are and what we do.

To contact me, email: jack (dot) king (at) manchester (dot) ac (dot) uk.

I am a member of the Association for Human Mathematics, and I do not use AI/LLMs in my research.
