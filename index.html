Welcome to my webpages. I am a researcher working in the School of Engineering at the University of Manchester. My research focusses on high-fidelity simulations of fluids. I work on both the development of novel numerical methods for a range of flow types, and also on the use of these (and established) methods to obtain new insights into fundamental physical behaviours. I have a particular focus on complex fluid problems which are challenging for conventional methods, and often require highly accurate or adaptive numerical schemes.

My research is currently funded by the Royal Society and the Leverhulme Trust. For publications: see Google Scholar or ArXiv. For information on my research, see research.

I co-lead the Complex Fluids Group at the University of Manchester - see our website for more details of who we are and what we do.
