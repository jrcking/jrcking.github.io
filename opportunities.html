Opportunities

We have several opportunities coming up in the next 12 months, at both PDRA and PhD level, which will be listed here as they arise. If you are interested in working with us, please email, to jack (dot) king (at) manchester (dot) ac (dot) uk.

Current advertised opportunities:

  • PhD in numerical investigations of turbulent flame-wall interactions. 4 years funding for home students.
  • PhD on enhancing mixing and reactions in porous media with viscoelastic instabilities. 4 years funding for home students.

Other opportunities:

We are always keen to hear from enthusiastic and talented students and research whose interests lie broadly within our areas of research (see our research page). If you are a researcher with a strong background in fluid dynamics and numerical methods, and an interest in combustion, turbulence, rheology or multiphase flows, please get in touch.

There are several sholarships and fellowships to which we can support applications: we are happy to mentor you to develop a proposal for one of these to join the group. If you'd like to pursue these opportunities, please get in touch by email.
